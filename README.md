# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (the "Health Hub") handles information. We take your privacy extremely seriously, especially when it concerns your health. This App is designed with a **"Privacy First"** approach: **all of your health, medication, and reminder data is stored strictly on your own device and is never transmitted to us.**

### 1. Information Collection and Storage (Local-Only)
The App is an offline tool. We do not operate external cloud servers to store your personal health metrics. 
* **Health and Medication Data:** Any information you enter into the App—including medicine names, brands, dosages, uses, side effects, and your medicine-taking reminder history—is stored **locally on your device's internal storage**. We have no access to this data, and it is never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third party.
* **Account Data:** The App does not require you to create an account, register, or log in. Your usage remains completely anonymous.

### 2. Device Permissions and How Data is Used
To provide features like the pharmacy finder and medication alerts, the App requires certain device permissions:
* **Precise Location Data:** To show you the closest pharmacies and clinics, the App requests access to your device's location services. Your location coordinates are processed in real-time to query mapping service providers (such as Google Maps or Apple Maps). This location data is never saved, tracked, or linked to an identity, and we never see it. You can disable location permissions in your device settings at any time.
* **Notifications / Reminders:** The App requests permission to send notifications to trigger your medicine-taking reminders. These alerts are managed entirely by your device’s local operating system and do not rely on an external push-notification server.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The App interacts with third-party application programming interfaces (APIs) only to execute specific core functions:
* **Map Providers:** When searching for clinics or pharmacies, map data is rendered via third-party services (like Apple Maps or Google Maps). Your device communicates directly with these providers, and their usage is governed by their respective privacy policies.
* **No Technical Analytics or Advertising:** We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or marketing analytics tools that track your behavior across the internet. 

### 4. Data Retention and Security
* **User Control & Deletion:** Because all data lives on your device, you have total control over it. You can modify or clear your data at any time within the App. 
* **App Uninstallation:** If you delete or uninstall the App from your device, all of your saved medication names, logs, and reminder settings will be permanently deleted. We cannot recover this data for you because we do not keep a backup.
* **Security Responsibility:** Because your data is stored locally, its security depends on the physical security of your device. We highly recommend securing your mobile device with a passcode, PIN, or biometric lock (FaceID/Fingerprint).

### 5. Children’s Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect or request data from anyone. Since all data is stored offline on the device, we do not have access to collect or store information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). 

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the App operates offline and cannot send you an email alert, any changes will be made available by updating the policy text directly within the App or on the App Store listing page. 

### 7. Important Medical Disclaimer
This App provides information regarding medication names, brands, dosages, uses, side effects, and first aid steps for educational and informational purposes only. **This App is not a medical device and does not provide professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.** Always consult a qualified healthcare professional or doctor before making any changes to your medication schedule or health routine.
